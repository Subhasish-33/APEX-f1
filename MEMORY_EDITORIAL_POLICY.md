# APEX-F1 Memory Editorial Governance
# Tier 5.5 — The Legacy Layer

## Core Philosophy
APEX-F1 is a canonical truth platform and an emotional memory experience. It is **NOT** tabloid motorsport media, a fan-edit YouTube channel, or a social-media engagement farm. 

The Memory Layer exists to preserve dignity, historical gravity, and emotional continuity. 

## 1. Respect for Tragedy
Motorsport is inherently dangerous. Tragic moments (loss of life, severe injury, or catastrophic championship collapses) must be treated with absolute reverence.

*   **Rule 1**: NEVER use "hype" or upbeat motion language (snapping, rapid cuts) around TRAGIC classifications. 
*   **Rule 2**: Tragic memory surfaces must default to extreme cinematic stillness. No looping background video.
*   **Rule 3**: NEVER use clickbait headlines for tragic moments. Use factual, culturally recognized titles (e.g., "Imola 1994", "Jules").

## 2. Anti-Sensationalism
APEX does not seek to artificially generate drama. The sport produces enough of it naturally.

*   **Rule 1**: Avoid algorithmic engagement bait phrasing ("You won't believe what happened on Lap 53"). Titles must be editorial, authoritative, and permanent (e.g., "The Title Decider", "Rain Masterclass").
*   **Rule 2**: CONTROVERSIAL moments (stewards decisions, team orders) must be presented neutrally but acknowledging the cultural weight. Do not take sides. State the ruling and the emotional fallout.

## 3. Radio Artifact Treatment
Radio moments are the human voice of the sport. They are sacred artifacts.

*   **Rule 1**: Do NOT treat radio like TikTok clips. 
*   **Rule 2**: Use analog, waveform-inspired typography or minimal visualizers. 
*   **Rule 3**: The transcript must fade in deliberately, forcing the user to *read* the emotion. 

## 4. Driver Mythology
We document careers, not just statistics.

*   **Rule 1**: Driver pages must reflect a career narrative ("The Unfinished Destiny" for Leclerc, "The Inevitable" for Verstappen). 
*   **Rule 2**: However, these archetypes must be derived from *data* and *historical consensus*, not arbitrary fan narratives. The `MemoryEngine` must determine the archetype based on the mathematical density of their memory classifications.

## 5. Temporal Echoes (Respecting Continuity)
When a live moment mirrors a past event, the platform should gently recall it.

*   **Rule 1**: Echoes must be subtle. Do not interrupt the live telemetry to show a 10-year-old memory. Present the echo as a footnote or an asynchronous "aside" during a decompression period (e.g., Safety Car).
